# Resource checklist

## Minimum viable start

- topic or content space;
- intended audience;
- output language and platform;
- vibe in plain language or one preset;
- image route: Flow Agent or buyer-supplied media;
- voice route: recording or configured TTS.

## Brand upgrade

- logo in SVG or transparent PNG;
- brand palette or a reference design;
- font files with commercial rights;
- pronunciation and voice guide;
- character or host footage;
- product/identity references;
- licensed music and SFX.

## Per episode

- selected idea or exact topic;
- current facts and source requirements;
- new product/person references when accuracy matters;
- approval of script and quota estimate;
- exact final voice file before timing.

## Rights checklist

Confirm the buyer may commercially use every supplied logo, face, product photo, character clip, font, music track, sound effect, and stock asset. Generated outputs may have separate provider terms. Keep credits and licenses in the episode source notes.
