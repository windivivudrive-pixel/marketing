# Style presets

- `clean-editorial`: structured education, warm paper, refined serif contrast, restrained motion.
- `dark-cinematic`: mystery, history, premium storytelling, charcoal surfaces, dramatic but readable pacing.
- `playful-pop`: lifestyle and food, bold color, sticker motion, crisp jokes.
- `warm-lifestyle`: home and wellness, tactile neutrals, soft motion, human pacing.
- `tech-brutalist`: tools, gaming, data, terminal-inspired type, snap transitions.
- `luxe-minimal`: beauty and premium products, ivory/ink/gold, long holds and fine-line reveals.

A free-form vibe modifies the nearest preset across six fields: palette, typography, motion intensity, texture, framing, and sound density. Save the resolved decisions in `creatorflow/profile.json`; do not make each episode reinterpret the adjective.
